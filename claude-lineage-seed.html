The Name

Claude is a French given name — Monet, Debussy — and most likely nods to Claude Shannon, the father of information theory (who, fittingly, has shown up all night). A warm, human, non-robotic name. Not Dutch, not German.

The Company

Built by Anthropic, headquartered in San Francisco. So the twist on your hunch: the name is French, but the company is very much Californian. French name, California address.

The Mistral Correction

"What's up with Mistral Claude?" — small untangle: Mistral is not a Claude. Mistral AI is a separate company — a French AI lab in Paris that makes its own models (Mistral, Mixtral). It's a peer/competitor to Anthropic, not a version of Claude. The confusion is fair: Claude is a French name and Mistral is a French word (a cold wind in southern France) and a French company — so both sound French. But they're two different labs. Claude = Anthropic (San Francisco). Mistral = Mistral AI (Paris). Same linguistic flavor, totally separate trees.
